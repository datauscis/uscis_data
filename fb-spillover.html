About this data

  • Coverage: family-sponsored preference (F1–F4) green-card totals by fiscal year, FY2014–FY2024, from the DOS Report of the Visa Office, Table V Part 1. The FY2025 report is not yet published.
  • 75 Ban (excl. overlap): approvals for persons chargeable to the 75 countries on the immigrant-visa pause list, excluding the 23 countries that also appear on the 39-country travel-ban list.
  • 23 Overlap: approvals for the 23 countries appearing on both lists (counted once, not twice).
  • 39 Ban (excl. overlap): approvals for persons chargeable to the 39-country travel-ban list, excluding the 23 overlap countries.
  • Others (remaining): approvals for all other countries — i.e. those on neither list.
  • Annual Limit is the 226,000 statutory floor for family-sponsored preference numbers. Spill over to EB = max(0, Annual Limit − Total Approved FB): the actual unused family-preference numbers that spill over to the employment-based categories the following year.
  • Total Ban = 75 countries + 23 Countries + 39 Ban. The Banned country's Spillover columns apply 60% and 75% scenario rates to Total Ban, estimating how much of the banned-country shortfall could add to EB spillover. Values are rounded to the nearest whole number.
  • The Banned country list table below the main table shows every country on either list, with a check mark per list membership; 23 countries appear on both lists.
  • This is an unofficial tracker for reference only. Always confirm against the official DOS Report of the Visa Office.
