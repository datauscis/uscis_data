About this data

  • Coverage: employment-based visa bulletins from Feb/2024 through Oct/2026 (33 bulletins) for India and China across EB-1, EB-2 and EB-3.
  • Final Action Dates come from the U.S. Department of State's monthly Visa Bulletin; Pending I-485 counts come from USCIS inventory data, as of each row's Final Action Date.
  • Movement is the change in the Final Action Date versus the previous bulletin, in days. Feb/2024 is the first row in this table, so it has no movement value.
  • India Pending Inventory is the USCIS pending I-485 inventory snapshot for India as of the bulletin date. An em dash (—) means no data was reported for that month; a hyphen (-) means the reported count was zero.
  • U = Unavailable — India EB-2 was unavailable from Jul/2026 through Sep/2026.
  • This is an unofficial tracker for reference only. Always confirm against the official Visa Bulletin.
